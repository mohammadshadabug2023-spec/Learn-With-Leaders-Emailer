Entrepreneurship Powered by AI

University of Salamanca

Turn ideas into business possibilities in one of Europe’s historic university cities. Learn how entrepreneurs research markets, understand finances and use AI thoughtfully.

Dates14–20 December 2026
Duration7 days / 6 nights
EligibilityGrades 8–12
Fee€3,250 per student

Cambridge, USA · 🩺

Pre-Med Horizons Programme

At Harvard University · By HUGEM

Explore the science, systems and social questions shaping healthcare—and work with others to propose solutions to a real-world health challenge.

Dates4–9 January 2027
FormatDay camp · 6 days
EligibilityGrades 8–12
FeeUS$1,500 per student
  • Human physiology, public health and healthcare systems
  • AI in health, vaccines, innovation and medical ethics
  • Team-based “Future of Health” capstone challenge
  • Lunch on programme days, workshops, outings and certificate included
Application deadline: 31 October 2026. No prior medical coursework is required.
